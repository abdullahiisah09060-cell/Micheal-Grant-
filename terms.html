Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this site, registering a secure account, or submitting any application for federal financial assistance, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be legally bound by these Terms and Conditions.

These terms constitute a binding legal agreement between the user ("Applicant") and the SBA. Access to federal funding is contingent upon continuous compliance with these regulations and all applicable provisions of the Code of Federal Regulations (CFR).

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for consideration under the 2025 Federal Grant Cycle, applicants must verify the following criteria through the portal's identity audit systems:

  • The applicant business must be physically located and operated within the 50 United States or U.S. territories.
  • The business must have been in active, verifiable operation for at least one (1) calendar year prior to application.
  • The entity must fall under the SBA size standards as defined by NAICS codes (typically under 500 employees).
  • The primary account holder must be a U.S. Citizen or Legal Permanent Resident aged 18 years or older.
  • The business must not have any delinquent federal debt, active federal debarment, or unresolved tax liens.

Account Security

Applicants are granted a single, non-transferable account for the management of grant requests. You are responsible for the absolute security of your credentials, including your multi-factor authentication (MFA) devices and your 4-digit Transaction PIN.

The SBA will never request your password or PIN via email or phone. Any unauthorized access resulting from the sharing of credentials is the sole liability of the user. We reserve the right to freeze any account suspected of compromised security.

Application Process

Submitting an application via Form 424-B constitutes a formal request for federal funds. Each submission undergoes a multi-stage audit: Automated Intake, Case Officer Review, and Treasury Authorization. The SBA reserves the right to request supplemental evidence, including tax transcripts and bank statements, at any stage.

Deposit and Fee Requirements

Large-scale disbursements (>$50,000) are subject to IRS Regulation §6325, requiring a Tax Clearance Certificate fee. This mandatory clearance ensures the recipient has no outstanding liabilities that would impede the award's federal purpose.

All fees must be settled through the authorized secure channels provided within the portal. Payments made to unauthorized external addresses are not recognized and will result in permanent disqualification.

Grant Disbursement Process

Once an application is approved and tax clearance is granted, the SBA will issue an official Certificate of Grant Award. Funds are disbursed to the Recipient's secure Treasury Wallet within the portal. This wallet acts as a controlled ledger for federal oversight until funds are moved to an external business account.

Withdrawal Policy

Withdrawals from the Treasury Wallet are subject to 24-hour cooling periods and manual review for anti-money laundering (AML) compliance. Users must verify the destination account (Routing/Account or Wallet Address). Federal wires are irreversible once broadcast. The SBA is not liable for funds lost due to incorrect destination data.

Prohibited Activities

The following activities result in permanent debarment and potential referral to the Department of Justice:

  • Submission of forged, digitally altered, or fraudulent identity documents.
  • Use of automated scripts, bots, or unauthorized scrapers to access portal data.
  • Providing false information regarding business revenue, employee count, or grant purpose.
  • Attempting to bypass the secure Tax Clearance or KYC protocols.

Privacy and Data

Data submitted is protected under the Privacy Act of 1974. We employ 256-bit AES encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 for transit. Information is shared only with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and the Department of the Treasury for the sole purpose of disbursement verification.

Dispute Resolution

Any disputes arising from this agreement shall be governed by U.S. Federal Law. The venue for any legal proceedings shall be the United States District Court for the District of Columbia. Resolution should first be attempted through the assigned SBA Case Officer.
