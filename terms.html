SBA Federal Portal Release 2025.1.0

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this Portal, registering an account, or submitting any application for federal financial assistance, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to be legally bound by these Terms. This constitutes a binding agreement between the Applicant and the U.S. Small Business Administration.

Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must verify they are a U.S.-based entity, in verifiable operation for at least 12 months, and meet the small business size standards. Use of a straw-man or fraudulent identity will result in permanent federal debarment.

Account Security

You are responsible for the absolute secrecy of your credentials and Transaction PIN. The SBA will never ask for your password via phone or email. Unauthorized access due to credential sharing is the sole liability of the user.

Fees & Tax Clearance

Disbursements over $50,000 are subject to IRS Regulation §6325. This requires a Tax Clearance Certificate fee to verify the absence of federal liens. Fees must be paid through authorized secure channels within the portal. External payments are not recognized.

Payout Policy

Withdrawals from the Treasury Wallet are Irreversible once broadcast. Payouts are subject to a 24-hour manual audit for compliance with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) standards. Ensure destination details are correct.

Data Privacy

Data submitted is protected under the Privacy Act of 1974. We employ AES-256 encryption. Information is shared only with the Department of the Treasury for disbursement verification purposes.
