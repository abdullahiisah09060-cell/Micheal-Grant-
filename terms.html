1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing or using the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) Grant Portal, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and all applicable federal laws and regulations. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, you must immediately cease use of the portal.

2. Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for grants through this portal, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • Be a legally registered business entity within the United States or its territories.
  • The applicant must be at least 18 years of age and authorized to bind the business entity.
  • Must not be currently debarred, suspended, or proposed for debarment by any Federal department or agency.
  • Must have a valid Employer Identification Number (EIN) or Social Security Number (SSN) for sole proprietorships.

3. Account Security

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials, including your password and Transaction PIN. Any activity performed through your account is deemed to be authorized by you. You must notify the SBA support team immediately if you suspect any unauthorized access.

4. Grant Process

Submission of an application does not guarantee funding. The SBA reserves the right to request additional documentation, perform site visits, or conduct background checks on business principles. Grants are awarded based on fund availability, program compliance, and business merit.

5. Disbursement Policy

Approved funds will be disbursed via the method selected in the user dashboard. The SBA is not responsible for funds sent to incorrect bank accounts or wallet addresses provided by the user. Users must ensure all banking and routing information is 100% accurate before requesting withdrawal.

6. Fees and Taxes

In accordance with federal tax regulations (IRS Section 6325), certain grant disbursements may require the payment of a Tax Clearance Certificate fee or processing fee. These fees are used to verify compliance and are separate from the grant award itself. Failure to comply with tax requirements will result in the suspension of fund disbursement.

7. Prohibited Activities

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Providing false, misleading, or fraudulent information on any application.
  • Using grant funds for activities not specified in the approved application.
  • Attempting to bypass security measures or gain unauthorized access to other user accounts.
  • Reverse engineering or scraping data from the portal.

8. Privacy & Data

The SBA collects and processes personal and business data in accordance with the Federal Privacy Act of 1974. Your data is encrypted and used solely for the purpose of grant administration and identity verification.
