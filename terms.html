Portal Regulations

SBA Federal Grant Portal Release 2025.1.0

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing this Portal and registering an account, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions and all applicable federal grant laws. These terms constitute a binding legal agreement between the Applicant and the U.S. Small Business Administration.

Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for consideration under the 2025 cycle, applicants must be U.S.-based entities in operation for at least 12 months. Use of straw-man identities or fraudulent business registration numbers will result in permanent federal debarment.

Fee Requirements

Disbursements exceeding $50,000 are subject to IRS Regulation §6325. This requires a Tax Clearance Certificate fee to ensure the business is free of federal liens. All fees must be settled via authorized secure settlement nodes within the portal.
