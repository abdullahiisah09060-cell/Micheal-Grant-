2026

1. Identity Verification

By proceeding, you authorize the SBA to conduct identity verification against federal databases. This is a mandatory step required before any grant disbursement can be processed. All submitted information is subject to federal records retention policies.

2. Disbursement Terms

Grant funds are provisioned to a secure account pending completion of required verification steps. To release funds, you must complete all profile steps within the specified filing period. Incomplete applications will be placed on administrative hold.

3. Anti-Fraud Policy

All sessions are recorded. Submitting false or misleading information in connection with a federal grant application is a violation of 18 U.S.C. § 1001 and may result in criminal prosecution, fines, or imprisonment.

4. Privacy & Data Use

Personal information collected through this portal is used solely for the purpose of processing your grant application. We do not sell or share your data with third parties except as required by federal law. Your data is protected in accordance with the Privacy Act of 1974.

5. Eligibility Confirmation

By submitting an application, you confirm that you meet all eligibility requirements, that all information provided is accurate and complete, and that you are authorized to apply on behalf of the business identified in your application.

6. Program Limitations

Grant awards are subject to available funding and program guidelines. Approval is not guaranteed. The SBA reserves the right to modify or terminate programs at any time in accordance with federal appropriations and regulatory requirements.
