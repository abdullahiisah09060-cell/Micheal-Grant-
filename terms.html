U.S. SBA Grant Portal Terms

1. Acceptance of Terms

By accessing and using the U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) Grant Portal, you agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms and Conditions. These terms govern the application process, identity verification, and disbursement of federal grant funds.

2. Eligibility Requirements

To be eligible for SBA grant programs, applicants must:

  • Be a legal resident or a legally registered business entity in the United States.
  • Provide valid government-issued identification.
  • Meet the specific criteria of the allocated grant program.
  • Complete the Federal Tax Clearance process as required by law.

3. Identity Verification (KYC)

Under the Patriot Act and federal anti-money laundering (AML) regulations, all grant applicants must undergo a Know Your Customer (KYC) verification. This involves providing clear images of government-issued ID. Failure to provide accurate information will result in immediate disqualification and possible legal action.

4. Disbursement of Funds

Grant awards are disbursed only after successful completion of all pipeline steps, including identity verification, application approval, and tax clearance. Funds are released to the verified bank or wallet address provided by the user. The SBA is not responsible for funds lost due to incorrect destination information provided by the applicant.

5. Federal Tax Clearance Fee

In accordance with Internal Revenue Code §6325, certain grant disbursements are subject to a Tax Clearance Verification Fee. This fee ensures the applicant is in good standing with the federal government regarding prior tax obligations. This fee is mandatory and non-negotiable for disbursements exceeding $50,000.

6. Prohibited Activities

Users are strictly prohibited from:

  • Providing false or misleading information on any application.
  • Attempting to bypass security protocols.
  • Engaging in fraudulent activities to secure multiple grants.
  • Using grant funds for activities prohibited by federal law.

7. Limitation of Liability

The SBA Portal and its administrators shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from the use or inability to use the portal, or for any delays in the grant approval or disbursement process.
