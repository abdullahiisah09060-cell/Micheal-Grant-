02 Eligibility Requirements

To apply for federal funding through this portal, applicants must meet the following criteria:

  • The business must be physically located and operated within the United States.
  • The business must have been in active operation for at least one (1) year.
  • The business must have fewer than 500 full-time employees.
  • The applicant must not be currently involved in bankruptcy proceedings.
  • The applicant must be in full compliance with federal tax obligations.

03 Account Registration and Security

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials, including passwords and Transaction PINs. You agree to:

  • Provide accurate, current, and complete information during registration.
  • Notify the SBA immediately of any unauthorized access to your account.
  • Accept full responsibility for all activities that occur under your account.

04 Grant Application Process

Submission of an application does not guarantee funding. Each application undergoes a rigorous multi-stage review process, including identity verification (KYC), business profile audit, and federal compliance screening. The SBA reserves the right to request additional documentation at any stage of the process.

05 Deposit and Fee Requirements

Certain grant programs may require a Tax Clearance Certificate fee or processing deposit as mandated by federal regulation §6325. These fees are used to verify compliance with the Internal Revenue Service (IRS) and ensure the recipient has no outstanding federal liens.

All deposits must be made through the secure channels provided within the portal. The SBA is not responsible for funds sent to unauthorized external addresses.

06 Grant Disbursement Process

Once an application is approved and tax clearance is granted, the SBA will issue a Certificate of Grant Award. Funds are disbursed to the user's treasury wallet within the portal. Users may then initiate a withdrawal to their verified personal or business accounts.

07 Withdrawal Policy

Withdrawals are subject to manual review by the Federal Treasury department. Payouts are typically processed within 24-72 business hours. Users must ensure that destination details (Bank, Zelle, or Wallet) are correct, as federal transfers cannot be reversed once authorized.

08 User Conduct and Prohibited Activities

You agree not to use the portal for any unlawful purpose or in any way that could damage, disable, or impair the service. Prohibited activities include, but are not limited to:

  • Submitting fraudulent or forged identity documents.
  • Attempting to bypass security protocols or hack other user accounts.
  • Using automated systems (bots) to access or crawl the portal.
  • Providing false financial statements during the application process.

09 Privacy and Data Protection

The SBA is committed to protecting your privacy. All personal and business data submitted to the portal is encrypted using 256-bit AES standards. Data is used solely for the purpose of grant eligibility assessment and federal reporting requirements.
