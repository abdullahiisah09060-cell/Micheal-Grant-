Federal Tax Clearance Verification

In accordance with IRS Regulation §6325, the Small Business Administration is required to verify the tax compliance of any business receiving federal awards exceeding $50,000. This mandatory clearance ensures the recipient has no outstanding federal liens.

Step Restricted

Tax clearance verification is only initiated after your Grant Application (Form 424-B) has been formally approved by the SBA Review Board.

Mandatory Clearance Fee: $0.00

This one-time administrative fee covers the federal processing and issuance of your electronic Tax Clearance Certificate.

Payments are processed through the secure treasury settlement portal.
