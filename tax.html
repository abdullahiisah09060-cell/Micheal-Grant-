Federal Tax Clearance Verification

In accordance with IRS Regulation §6325, the Small Business Administration is required to verify the tax compliance of any business receiving federal awards exceeding $50,000. This clearance ensures the recipient has no outstanding federal liens or liabilities that would impede the award's purpose.

Step Not Available

Tax clearance verification is only initiated after your Grant Application (Form 424-B) has been formally approved.

Required Clearance Fee: $0.00

Pay this fee to generate your electronic Tax Clearance Certificate for disbursement.

Tax clearance payments are processed through the secure treasury portal.
